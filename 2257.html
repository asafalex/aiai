18 U.S.C. § 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements Compliance Statement

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Naked Woman Site (https://nakedwoman.site/) is an adult-oriented website. This statement describes our compliance with United States record-keeping requirements under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75, where applicable.

1. Nature of content on this website

Visual depictions accessible on this website are produced through automated artificial-intelligence and computer-generated imagery (“CGI”) tools based on text prompts submitted by users. They are not photographs or recordings of real, identifiable human beings. No actual persons perform in sexually explicit conduct on this website.

2. Age of depicted persons

We prohibit prompts or content that depict, imply, or could reasonably be interpreted as depicting minors in a sexual or exploitative manner. We do not knowingly publish sexually explicit visual depictions of persons who were under eighteen (18) years of age at the time the depiction was created.

3. Applicability of § 2257 record-keeping

Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 2257(h)(2)(B) and applicable regulations, the record-keeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75 do not apply to visual depictions that do not consist of depictions of actual human beings engaged in actual sexually explicit conduct. Because the sexually explicit visual content on this website consists of AI-generated and computer-generated imagery rather than depictions of actual human performers, those federal record-keeping requirements are not applicable to such content.
